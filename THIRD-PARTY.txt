=================================

EuropaVR itself - dsound.dll, EuropaVR.dll, the Lua script and the scripts in the
repository - is MIT licensed. See LICENSE.


WHAT IS NOT BUNDLED, AND WHY
----------------------------
UEVR is "All rights reserved" (https://github.com/praydog/UEVR/blob/master/LICENSE).
Only its include/ directory carries a separate MIT licence, and that directory
states so explicitly.

So:

  * EuropaVR.dll is built against UEVR's include/ headers, which are MIT. That
    notice is in licenses\UEVR-SDK-LICENSE.txt.

  * UEVRBackend.dll and UEVRPluginNullifier.dll are NOT redistributed here. They
    are praydog's work and not ours to hand out. Download UEVR yourself from
    https://github.com/praydog/UEVR/releases and copy them in, as INSTALL.txt
    explains.

  * openvr_api.dll (Valve, BSD-3-Clause) and openxr_loader.dll (The Khronos
    Group, Apache-2.0) are permissively licensed and could be redistributed, but
    they come in the same UEVR download, so they are left there too rather than
    split across two sources.

If a build ever does bundle those binaries - package.ps1 -IncludeUevr does, for
local testing - then the licences\ folder carries their licence texts, and such a
build must not be published without permission from praydog.
